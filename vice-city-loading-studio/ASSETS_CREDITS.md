## Sample photos

`public/samples/1005.jpg`, `1011.jpg`, `1027.jpg` are placeholder portrait
samples used only so the editor has something to load and the exported canvas
stays same-origin (no CORS taint). **Before public submission, confirm each
sample's source and license, or replace them with self-owned or CC0 photos.**
Users' own uploads are read locally as data URLs and never leave the browser.
